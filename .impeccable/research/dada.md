# Dada (1916–1924): research dossier, condensed

Sources: Tate glossary, Centre Pompidou (Hausmann ABCD, fmsbwtözäu), International Dada Archive (Iowa), Monoskop, UbuWeb (Richter), MIT Docubase (DADA-DATA), Met (Man Ray), MoMA/NGA via search summaries.

## Heart → page rules
- Anti-art, provocation: the page mocks portfolio conventions while still being one. Heckling labels may sit beside the real heading in plain type ("Dada is against the future, Dada is dead, Dada is idiotic, Long live Dada!", Kleine Dada Soirée 1922). Slogans are decoration, never labels.
- Chance (Arp's squares "according to the laws of chance" 1916–17; Tzara's 1920 cut-up poem recipe): chance controls position, angle and which decoration appears; never reading order or content. A chance result can be redrawn.
- Simultaneity (Hausmann's ABCD): one decorative layer runs alongside the content (stamps, ticket stubs, slogan fragments); the content column stays single and linear.
- Sound poetry (Ball's Karawane, Hausmann's poster poems e.g. fmsbwtözäu 1918, Schwitters' Ursonate "Fümms bö wö tää zää Uu"): letters are sound and shape; display type breaks words into phonemes at different sizes. Interface sounds are syllables, not beeps.
- Readymade (Duchamp Fountain 1917 "R. Mutt", L.H.O.O.Q. 1919): UI built from found printed matter: tickets as buttons, rubber stamps as tags, torn receipts as dividers, a defaced printed form as CV. Sly readymade captions.
- Machine absurdity (Picabia mechanomorphs in 291, Hausmann's Mechanical Head): skills as labelled machine diagrams, never progress bars.

## Works and their grammar
- Höch, Cut with the Kitchen Knife (1919–20): halftone photos at mismatched scales, clean hard scissor cuts, gears/ball bearings, small pasted words, rough diagonal sweep.
- Hausmann: ABCD (letters from the mouth, ticket, flyer); poster poems in mixed woodtype across a wide coloured sheet.
- Schwitters, Merz: small collages of tram tickets, receipts, wrappers, stamps; layered rectangles at slight angles; word fragments ("Merz" from "Commerz- und Privatbank").
- Tzara manifestos: mixed typefaces within a paragraph, bold caps for emphasis; cut-up words on mismatched baselines.
- Ball, Karawane (Dada Almanach 1920): a different face per line; cardboard bishop costume.
- Arp: flat coloured paper rectangles in a near-grid, slightly off axis, quiet colours, lots of space (the calm counterweight).
- Duchamp, Rotoreliefs (1935, spun ~33 rpm, illusion of depth); Anémic Cinéma (1926, spiral discs + spiral puns).
- Picabia: thin technical machine drawings on bare paper with deadpan labels; 391 covers with one line drawing.
- Heartfield: crisp political photomontage; Dada Almanach (black + green letterpress); Der Dada 3 cover.
- Van Doesburg & Schwitters, Kleine Dada Soirée poster (1922–23): big red DADA in several orientations overprinted on black type, random capitals, odd angles, manicules, small vignettes. Best single typography reference.
- Der Dada: "a multiplicity of opposing fonts and signs".
- Man Ray rayographs (1921–22): white object silhouettes on black.

## Type
Several faces, sizes, weights in one line; words rotated 90/180°; woodtype with uneven ink; typewriter; rubber stamps; manicules, heavy rules, printers' ornaments; bold sans caps beside a Didone italic.
Google Fonts (verified): woodtype **Holtwood One SC**, Ultra, Alfa Slab One, Six Caps, Stint Ultra Expanded; grotesques **Archivo Black**, Anton, Anybody (variable width); Didones **Abril Fatface**, Bodoni Moda, Libre Bodoni; body **Old Standard TT**; typewriter **Special Elite**, Courier Prime; blackletter UnifrakturMaguntia (sparingly, as the mocked establishment face).
Mixing rules: body text one face only (Old Standard TT or Courier Prime), upright, normal measure. Disruption only in headings and decorative layers: a face per word/syllable from a fixed set of four (Holtwood, Abril Fatface italic, Archivo Black, Special Elite), sizes 1.0×/0.6×/1.8× on a shared baseline, at most one rotated word per heading, red on the single loudest word only. Manicule (☞, drawn as SVG) as link affordance. Avoid Rye/Smokum/Sancreek (saloon, not Zurich).

## Colour and material
Newsprint #EDE6D3, aged paper #E3D3A8, foxing #C9B27C, letterpress black #1A1814, vermilion #D22B1F, oxblood #9E1B14, ticket green #5E7F5A, poster-poem orange #E07A2E, stamp violet-blue #3B4A8C, Arp papers #8C7B5A / #B9AC90.
Halftone on every photo (coarse screen); red registered 1–2px off the black; torn edges only on paper fragments, hard cut edges on photomontage; faint fold lines, show-through, ticket perforations, circular postmarks. Body text full contrast #1A1814 on #EDE6D3.

## Composition
One readable spine plus an unruly margin. Elements meet at 3–12° (never 45°). Layers overprint. Mismatched scale. Plenty of empty paper.
Layout ideas: intro as the Soirée poster (typewriter column; huge red DADA rotated behind; the sections listed as a printed "programme"; manicules to the action); work as a Der Dada newspaper spread (each job a news item with Didone headline + dateline; clippings and stamps of tech names between); projects as a Merz board (collage cards: halftone image, ticket stub with year, receipt listing the stack; chance tilt; focus straightens and lifts); skills as a Picabia machine portrait (each part labelled with a skill, plus a plain list); contact as a typed telegram form with a red stamp.

## Motion (printed paper and mechanical devices: hard stops, steady linear spins, 8–12fps stepped cut-paper moments, no glossy easing)
1. Arp drop entry: paper squares fall with slight drift, land at random angle ≤8°, settle 400–600ms, no bounce; a "shake" control re-drops for a new layout.
2. Tzara's hat headline: heading breaks into word slips that fall into a hat, come out in random order ~1.2s, reassemble. DOM text never changes.
3. Ransom-letter typing: each letter arrives in a different face and size with jitter and a soft thunk.
4. Rotorelief loader/transition: spiral disc at 33 rpm (~1.8s/turn, linear); a full-screen disc spins up into the next view; pun text around the rim.
5. Rubber-stamp hover/press: tickets as buttons; a red stamp ("JA", "DADA", "GENEHMIGT") swings in on hover; on press it lands with 1px squash, misregistered ink spread fading 150ms.
6. Manicule cursor: pointing hand; another engraved hand over links.
7. Rhythmus 21 dividers: white rectangles on black grow, shrink, slide with scroll (dividers only).
8. Eggeling lines: white lines draw across black, build a title, dissolve.
9. Ghosts Before Breakfast: bowler hats float across contact and flee the cursor; sending plays it in reverse.
10. Ursonate sound: syllables at random pitches (our synth does formant syllables).
11. Overprint misregistration on hover: red layer slips 2–4px off the black and back in 200ms.
12. Readymade signature: clicking an image adds "R. Mutt 1917" or a pencilled moustache with a caption like "now it's art".
Reduced motion: settled collage, no drops or spins.

## Clichés to avoid
1990s grunge (distressed fonts, coffee stains, duct tape, spray paint); Wild West woodtype; chaos applied evenly everywhere (real Dada has empty space and clear focus); rotating everything including body text; torn edges everywhere; Surrealist imagery; glitch/vaporwave as "anti-art"; pasting copyrighted works (Höch, Duchamp, Heartfield); absurdity without wit.
