## Direction contract

THESIS: One person hung as four art movements. The visitor picks a room, and the same true story (who I am, where I've been, what I can do, what I've made, what's next) is rebuilt in that movement's own practice. Refuses the category default of one themed page with a palette swap, and refuses movement pastiche (primary confetti, POW bubbles, grunge collage, low-poly).

OWN-WORLD: Gallery: a warm night wall #0f0e0d with four lit canvases, Archivo for chrome. Bauhaus: paper #EDE7D9, ink, vermilion bars, Archivo expanded lowercase, rails and diagonals. Dada: newsprint #EDE6D3, letterpress black and vermilion, per-word mixed woodtype/Didone/typewriter, stamps and tickets. Pop: flat silkscreen inks on #F4EEDC, Ultra and Anton, misregistered black key, serial grids. Cubism: canvas #E8DCC4, ochre/umber/grey-blue planes with open passage edges, stencil caps over a newspaper roman, colour earned toward Synthetic.

STORY: The gallery says: four movements, one person, pick a room. Each room carries all of it, leading with its emphasis: Bauhaus who I am, Dada where I've been, Pop what I can do, Cubism what's next. Every room ends at a way to reach me (email, cal.com, GitHub, LinkedIn, X).

FIRST VIEWPORT: Gallery: four full-height canvases side by side on the wall, each a live composition in its movement with its name in its own type; the hovered one widens, performs and plays its theme; a thin chrome strip carries devayu and the sound control. Phones: four stacked bands. Rooms: the hero is the movement's scene only, never the name; "I'm Ayush" starts below it.

FORM: Select screen into four whole rooms, user-pinned structure (the user rejected the one-scroll journey and the roll for this). seed: none (unavailable)

Signature interaction: the rooms' movement-native micro-interactions (Bauhaus rails and hinges, Dada chance and stamps, Pop silkscreen pulls and misregistration, Cubist viewpoint shifts), and covers between rooms made of the destination's material, with the music crossfading on the beat.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
