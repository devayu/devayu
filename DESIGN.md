# Design System: devayu

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Gallery of Four Rooms"**

devayu is one gallery shell and four whole rooms. The shell is a warm night wall with four lit canvases on it and almost no chrome; its job is to stay out of the way. Each room is a complete, separate visual world built from its movement's practice (type, materials, composition, motion), and each one rebuilds the same true story from the same data in `src/data/`. Nothing is a palette swap over a shared template: rooms do not share faces, colors, buttons or easing. What they share is structure: a world mount that marks `html[data-world]` and binds Esc to the gallery, cover transitions made of the destination's material, one sound-control contract, one content model, and one craft floor.

Density runs high inside the rooms (posters, newspapers, shelves, still lifes) and low on the wall. Every room's hero is the movement's scene only, with no name; "I'm Ayush" begins below it. Every room ends at a way to reach him. The rejected modes, confirmed by the direction contract, are the one-page-with-a-theme-swap default and movement pastiche (primary confetti, comic POW bubbles, grunge collage, low-poly).

**Key Characteristics:**
- One shell, four sovereign worlds; each room's tokens are scoped to its root class and never leak.
- Every composition is original SVG/CSS from the movement's grammar; no reproduced artworks, photos or brands.
- Motion is the movement's own mechanism: Bauhaus cams on rails, Dada stepped chance, Pop mechanical print pulls, Cubist viewpoint shifts.
- Sound is a design material: four generative themes on one 104 bpm transport, crossfading on the beat between rooms.
- Phones are first-class: the wall becomes four stacked bands, every room has a dedicated phone pass.

## Colors

Five palettes that never mix: a night-wall neutral for the gallery, and one movement palette per room, each anchored on its own warm paper ground.

### Primary
- **Bauhaus Vermilion** (bauhaus-vermilion): the room's one structural accent. The full-height bar beside the hero, Bayer's link bar, the focus ring, heading top-bars, the rail's active stripe.
- **Dada Vermilion** (dada-vermilion): the second printing. The misregistered shadow behind black headings, stamps, link underlines, the focus ring, selection.
- **Pop Red** (pop-red): the default ink screen of every printed heading, under the black key.
- **Cubism Ochre** (cubism-ochre): the analytic plane colour and the default face of every facet button.

### Secondary
- **Bauhaus Yellow and Ultramarine** (bauhaus-yellow, bauhaus-ultramarine): only on the stage (hero) and in the building kit; with Bauhaus Pink and Green as kit and seal accents.
- **Dada Inks** (dada-orange, dada-green, dada-violet and their pale tints): ticket stock and pasted scraps; violet is the rubber-stamp ink.
- **Pop Silkscreen Inks** (pop-yellow, pop-pink, pop-cyan, pop-turquoise, pop-orange, pop-lime, pop-blue): flat serial-grid cells, tags and boxes. Yellow is also the highlight behind hovered links and the focus halo.
- **Cubism Analytic Planes** (cubism-sienna, cubism-umber, cubism-grey, cubism-gris): the restricted palette of the top of the room; umber is the default hard plane edge.

### Tertiary
- **Cubism Synthetic and Orphic** (cubism-red, cubism-cobalt, cubism-yellow, cubism-orange, cubism-violet, cubism-emerald, cubism-sky): earned on the way down the page; the projects strip, the what's-next lamp and the closing poster.

### Neutral
- **Night Wall** (gallery-wall): the gallery ground, cover wall and default document background.
- **Gallery Bone** (gallery-ink): gallery text, the gate's primary button, selection.
- **Gallery Dim** (gallery-dim): secondary gallery copy only.
- **Bauhaus Paper / Buff / Card** (bauhaus-paper, bauhaus-buff, bauhaus-card): page, section band, book-spine card stock. **Bauhaus Ink** (bauhaus-ink): text, the rail, the heaviest bars. **Bauhaus Grey**: thin construction rails.
- **Dada Newsprint / Aged / Sheet / Slip** (dada-newsprint, dada-aged, dada-sheet, dada-slip): grained page, aged tickets and poster, newspaper sheet, paper slips. **Dada Ink / Soft Ink / Oxblood**: letterpress black, faded captions, link hover.
- **Pop Paper** (pop-paper) and **Pop Key** (pop-key): the unprinted stock and the black key plate (text, nav strip, outlines).
- **Cubism Ground / Chalk / News / Paper Green** (cubism-ground, cubism-chalk, cubism-news, cubism-paper-green): canvas, chalk planes, pasted newspaper, flock wallpaper. **Cubism Charcoal / Ink**: hard edges, focus ring, text.

### Named Rules
**The Sealed Room Rule.** A room's colours are declared on its root (`.bh-room`, `.dd-root`, `.pp-room`, `.cb-room`) and on `html[data-world=…]`; nothing room-specific may live in `globals.css`. A new surface in a room uses that room's tokens only.

**The Earned Colour Rule.** Cubism starts analytic (ochre, umber, greys on canvas) and only reaches full Synthetic colour as the visitor scrolls (the ground is mixed by scroll progress). Saturated Cubist colour does not appear in the hero.

**The One Vermilion Rule.** In Bauhaus and Dada, vermilion is the only hot colour carrying structure; Bauhaus yellow and ultramarine stay on the stage and the kit.

## Typography

**Gallery Font:** Archivo (variable width) for all chrome.
**Bauhaus:** Archivo at 125% width, weight 700, lowercase, for headings; Work Sans for text; Jost for numerals and the rail.
**Dada:** per-word mixed printings: Holtwood One SC (woodtype), Abril Fatface and Bodoni Moda italic (fat Didone), Archivo Black (black grotesk), Special Elite (typewriter); Old Standard TT for text.
**Pop Art:** Ultra (fat Clarendon) for logotypes and the intro name; Anton for printed headings, tags and labels; Familjen Grotesk for back-of-pack text.
**Cubism:** Big Shoulders Stencil caps for chrome, buttons and sprayed hero words; Stardos Stencil for section headings; Old Standard TT for newspaper-roman text.

**Character:** Each room speaks in its movement's actual type practice, and every face is loaded on demand (`preload: false`), so a room pays only for its own type. The gallery labels each canvas in that room's own display face.

### Hierarchy
- **Display**: Bauhaus name block (bauhaus-display, line-height 0.8, stacked); Pop intro name in Ultra (pop-display); Cubism closing and next words in stencil caps (cubism-display); the gallery gate title (gallery-display).
- **Headline**: section heads per room (bauhaus-headline hanging from a top bar; dada-headline cut from mixed printings with a red second printing; pop-headline printed red with an offset black key; cubism-headline in mixed-case Stardos Stencil).
- **Body**: Bauhaus 1rem/1.5 lowercase with ledes at 44ch; Dada 1.125rem/1.6; Pop 1.0625rem/1.55, intro paragraphs at 1.125rem within 62ch; Cubism 18px/1.6 with italic ledes at 54ch.
- **Label**: Bauhaus Jost 500 vertical rail labels; Dada Special Elite on tickets and hats; Pop Anton uppercase at 0.04em on tags; Cubism Big Shoulders Stencil 800 uppercase at 0.16em on facets.

### Named Rules
**The Lowercase Rule.** Everything in the Bauhaus room is lowercase, set by the room root; never reintroduce capitals there.

**The Mixed Printing Rule.** Dada headings are built word by word from different faces (and ransom letters on chance), never one face for a whole heading.

**The Own-Face Label Rule.** On the gallery wall, each canvas names itself in its room's display face; the wall's own chrome is Archivo only.

## Layout

The gallery is a three-row viewport grid (chrome, canvases, foot) at 100svh with a 560px floor: 14px/16px edges, 12px row gap, four canvases in a flex row 10px apart. Hovering or focusing a canvas widens it (flex-grow 2.3 against 0.75) and dims the others (saturate 0.6, brightness 0.62). Below 767px the wall becomes four stacked bands of max(150px, (100svh − 150px)/4), labels move to the right 62%, taglines hide, and the page scrolls.

Rooms each own their spatial grammar:
- **Bauhaus:** a fixed 84px ink rail on the left (vertical nav, sound dial), content in a flush-left grid with a fluid gutter (clamp(20px, 5.5vw, 96px)); diagonals at 30°; phones collapse at 760px.
- **Dada:** a full poster in the first viewport, then a broadsheet with columns and folds, a machine diagram, a Merz board and a telegram; tickets scroll horizontally in the nav; breakpoints at 900px and 720px.
- **Pop:** a fixed 52px black channel strip; a full-bleed serial grid hero; billboard-scale headings; shelves and a horizontal panorama; breakpoints at 1100px and 680px.
- **Cubism:** a still life in planes; an oval canvas for the intro; Gris-style grid for work; a simultaneous strip for projects; content columns cap near 1180px; breakpoints at 900px and 720px.

Rhythm is per room; there is no shared spacing scale beyond the 44px minimum touch target.

## Elevation & Depth

There is no shared elevation system. Depth belongs to each world's material.

- **Gallery:** canvases hang on the wall with one deep ambient drop and a spotlight from above.
- **Bauhaus:** flat. Depth comes only from overlap and bars; no shadows.
- **Dada:** physical paper. Scraps, tickets and slips sit slightly off the page with small soft lifts, and lift further when picked up; stamps print as inset vermilion rings.
- **Pop:** misregistration, not elevation. Hard zero-blur offsets are the black key plate landing off-register, always down-right on the key, and they collapse to zero on press.
- **Cubism:** passage. Planes carry one or two hard charcoal or umber edges while their other edges feather into the next plane; hover bleeds the open edges wider and darkens the hard edge. Facet buttons show a hatched shadow side that jumps across on hover.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Wall hang** (`box-shadow: 0 34px 60px -34px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.9)`): gallery canvases only.
- **Paper rest** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgb(26 24 20 / 0.16)`): Dada scraps and slips at rest.
- **Paper lifted** (`box-shadow: 0 10px 14px -8px rgb(26 24 20 / 0.32), 0 2px 4px rgb(26 24 20 / 0.08)`): a Dada scrap picked up on hover.
- **Pop key** (`box-shadow: 4px 3px 0 var(--key)`; hover `8px 6px 0`; press `0 0 0`): Pop buttons and boxes; headings carry the same offset as a `::before` key layer.

### Named Rules
**The Material Depth Rule.** Depth is always a material behaviour of the room (wall light, paper lift, print register, passage), never a generic card shadow.

**The Register Rule.** Pop's black key lands off-register by (+4px, +3px) by default; this hard offset is Pop's print grammar and belongs to Pop alone.

## Shapes

Square by default everywhere: the system has no radius scale. Round forms appear only where the object is round: Bauhaus circles and the sound dial, Pop dots and tag holes, Cubism's sound disc and pins, the gallery sound dot. Each room has its own cut:
- **Bauhaus:** the three primitives (circle, triangle, square) morph by clip-path on interaction; bars and rails are hard rectangles.
- **Dada:** perforated ticket edges via radial masks, torn paper edges via polygon clips, rotated slips (±1–3°).
- **Pop:** price-tag notches, the Oldenburg squash button as the one soft blob, the black-keyed square tiles of the serial grid.
- **Cubism:** irregular quadrilateral planes (`polygon(3% 0, 100% 7%, 97% 100%, 0 90%)` on facets), an oval canvas.

## Components

### Gallery canvas (signature)
- **Character:** a lit canvas on a night wall that performs its movement when chosen.
- **Shape:** square-cornered, full-height, wall hang shadow, spotlight overlay.
- **States:** hover/focus widens it over 760ms (cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)), plays its theme and runs its movement (Bauhaus parts slide on a 30° rail; Dada stamp drops and the hand points in steps; Pop squeegee pulls and the key jumps register; Cubism planes slide to another viewpoint). Tagline fades in 220ms after. Focus ring is 2px Gallery Bone at 4px offset.
- **Entry:** the four canvases hang in with a 70ms stagger.

### Buttons
- **Gallery:** the gate's primary is a Bone slab with Wall text, 52px tall, lifts 2px on hover and sinks 1px on press; the quiet option is an underlined text button.
- **Bauhaus:** links and actions use Bayer's bar: a thick vermilion bar runs out from the left under the text on hover and retracts to the right on leave (180ms cam), turning ink on press.
- **Dada:** hats and actions are ink-outlined typewriter labels, rotated −3°, inverting to ink on hover in two steps.
- **Pop:** solid ink blocks with the key offset; hover pushes the key further out, press drops the block onto it.
- **Cubism:** facet buttons: irregular plane, stencil caps, a hatched shadow side on the left. Hover moves the light (face lightens a step, hatching jumps right) in a hard 150ms step; press sinks 2px into the shadow. Variants: ochre default, chalk quiet, red, yellow, charcoal reach.

### Navigation
Every room has a way back to the gallery and to the other three rooms, plus its sound control.
- **Bauhaus:** the ink rail: vertical Jost labels, each room marked by its primitive; a vermilion stripe scales in on hover.
- **Dada:** tram tickets (number, room name in its own face, years) with perforated edges and slight tilts, plus a gramophone tag for sound.
- **Pop:** a black channel strip of price tags; each tag's ink is its own screen that slides off the key on hover.
- **Cubism:** cut chalk planes along the top in stencil caps, with a charcoal "reach me" plane at the right.

### Sound control (shared contract)
Every room carries one sound toggle marked `data-sound-toggle` with `aria-pressed` and a label that names the state, dressed in the room's object: the gallery's pulsing dot (pulses on two beats at 104 bpm), the Bauhaus dial, the Dada gramophone tag, the Pop jingle tag, the Cubism disc.

### Covers between rooms (signature)
Moving between rooms runs a full-screen cover made of the destination's material, while the music crossfades. Bauhaus: a Triadic Ballet costume tumbles in 90° steps and paper parts along a rail. Dada: a spinning Rotorelief card torn in two. Pop: two silkscreen pulls, then a channel flip. Cubism: five planes stepping down a diagonal, then sliding apart. Back to the gallery: wall panels drop in with a 40ms stagger. Reduced motion skips the cover.

### Section headings
Each room's heading component carries its practice: Bauhaus headings hang from a top bar; Dada headings are cut from four printings with a red second printing that shifts on hover; Pop headings are pulled in as an ink screen then the black key, with a squeegee bar crossing; Cubism stencil letters are sprayed in 30ms apart.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** scope every new rule to its room root or `html[data-world]`; CSS stays loaded after client-side moves between rooms.
- **Do** build each room's micro-interactions from its movement's mechanism: rails and hard-stop cams (cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.9, 0.1, 1)) in Bauhaus, stepped chance (steps(2–3)) in Dada, mechanical steps (steps(2, jump-none), 90ms) in Pop, hard 150ms light steps and feathering passage in Cubism.
- **Do** keep heroes as the scene only; start the name and introduction below.
- **Do** give every room a dedicated phone pass and keep touch targets at 44px or more.
- **Do** provide a reduced-motion state where every print, cut and plane is already in place.
- **Do** draw icons and arrows as authored SVG in the room's own stroke (Cubism arrows: one 1.75 stroke, square caps, open head).

### Don't:
- **Don't** let one room's palette, face or easing appear in another room; the gallery is the only place all four meet, each inside its own canvas.
- **Don't** reproduce real artworks, artists' images or real brands; compose from the movement's grammar.
- **Don't** fall into movement pastiche: primary confetti, comic POW bubbles, grunge collage, low-poly.
- **Don't** use Unicode glyphs or icon fonts as icons.
- **Don't** add capitals to the Bauhaus room or a single uniform face to a Dada heading.
- **Don't** put saturated Synthetic colour at the top of the Cubism room.
